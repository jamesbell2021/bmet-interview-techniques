Loadout for the real thing

  • Three STAR stories, written out. Situation, Task, Action, Result — from your own projects, ready before you sit down.
  • One decision you reversed. A design you changed after a playtest proves more than a design that worked first time.
  • A two-minute critique. One game you love, one system in it, one specific change and its trade-off.
  • Two questions for them. About the team's process, not the perks.
